# OWC Introduction

## Welcome to Onboard Wireless Connectivity (OWC)

Welcome to the Onboard Wireless Connectivity (OWC) system – the foundational platform that enables all ScanReach solutions to operate seamlessly on your vessel. As an installer, whether you're a ship crew member or IT professional, this manual will guide you through the complete installation process of the OWC system, which serves as the backbone for ConnectPOB, ConnectFuel, ConnectCCM, and other ScanReach applications.

## What is OWC?

Onboard Wireless Connectivity (OWC) is ScanReach's breakthrough wireless IoT platform designed specifically for the maritime industry. This innovative technology enables personnel and asset control in complex and confined steel environments without the need for complex and expensive cabling. The OWC system creates a wireless mesh network throughout your vessel that serves as the communication infrastructure for all ScanReach solutions.

## Our Mission

At ScanReach, our mission is to increase personnel safety, vessel performance, and contribute to a more sustainable future. The OWC system is the foundation that makes this possible by providing reliable, wireless connectivity in the challenging maritime environment where traditional wireless technologies often fail.

## Why OWC Matters

The OWC system is the critical first step in implementing any ScanReach solution because:

- **It eliminates the need for complex cabling** throughout the vessel
- **It provides reliable connectivity** in steel environments where traditional wireless systems struggle
- **It enables all ScanReach solutions** to operate effectively
- **It's designed for maritime conditions** with robust, durable components
- **It's scalable and flexible** to accommodate vessels of all sizes and types

## Functionality

### Instant overview of approximate location

OWC with ConnectPOB gives an instant overview of onboard personnel, allowing immediate and high-precision rescue team involvement. It includes a setup of hardware devices, a computer and a virtual dashboard listed and explained below:

- Personal wearables
- Vessel interior and exterior sensors, mesh nodes
- Gateway (central unit for processing data)
- Dashboard, easily accessible on existing screens on the bridge
- Safety and alarm tablet

### Functional criteria

OWC has some criteria to secure full-time functionality, described thoroughly in the user manual. The basic requirements for installing and running the system are as follows:

- Powering the server onboard with UPS backup power, which ensures system uptime in case of a power blackout
- Internet connection for 2-ways communication to ScanReach cloud services. Sharing data to the cloud, system maintenance and updates in return
- Access to integrated resources on the local area network if configured
- Internal Wi-Fi access is preferable on UPS. Tablet is connected to ship Wi-Fi
- Mesh nodes will be located at the convenience of the nearest power outlet. Runs on net-power and includes a backup battery
