# Wearable Admin Page

**Purpose:** Manage all wearable tracking devices used by personnel, including assignment, monitoring, and maintenance of these critical safety devices.

## What You Can Do:

### Wearable Inventory

- **View all wearables:** See complete inventory of all tracking devices
- **Device status monitoring:** Check battery levels, signal strength, and connectivity
- **Search wearables:** Find specific devices by MAC address or assigned person
- **Sort by criteria:** Order devices by status, battery level, or assignment

### Device Assignment

- **Assign to personnel:** Connect wearables to specific crew members
- **Unassign devices:** Remove assignments when people leave or devices change
- **View assignments:** See who is currently assigned to each device
- **Assignment history:** Track previous assignments for each device

### Status Monitoring

- **Battery levels:** Monitor power status of all devices
- **Connectivity status:** See which devices are communicating properly
- **Signal strength:** Check communication quality with the network
- **Temperature monitoring:** Track device operating temperatures

### Device Management

- **Enable/disable devices:** Take devices in/out of service
- **Replacement tracking:** Manage device swaps and replacements
- **Maintenance scheduling:** Track when devices need servicing
- **Device lifecycle:** Monitor device age and usage patterns

### Filtering and Search

- **Filter by status:** Show only connected, disconnected, or low-battery devices
- **Filter by assignment:** View assigned vs unassigned devices
- **Search capabilities:** Find devices by various criteria including MAC address
- **Sort options:** Organize list by different device properties

### Bulk Operations

- **Select multiple devices:** Use checkboxes to work with groups of wearables
- **Bulk status changes:** Enable/disable multiple devices at once
- **Mass configuration:** Apply settings to multiple devices simultaneously

### Reporting and Analytics

- **Device statistics:** Overview of total devices, assignments, and status counts
- **Performance metrics:** Track device reliability and performance trends
- **Usage reporting:** Generate reports on device utilization
