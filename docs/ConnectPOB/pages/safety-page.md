# Safety Page

**Purpose:** The Safety page is the primary emergency and safety management center for the vessel. It provides real-time tracking of personnel during normal operations and emergency situations like mustering events.

## What You Can Do:

### Map Operations

- **View vessel layout:** See an interactive map showing the ship's structure with all areas and nodes
- **Track personnel locations:** View real-time positions of crew members throughout the vessel
- **Monitor emergency events:** During emergencies, see distress signals and mustering activities

### Personnel Tracking

- **View personnel counts:** See how many people are in each area of the ship
- **Monitor rescue personnel:** Track the location of designated rescue team members
- **Handle distress situations:** Respond to emergency alerts from crew members

### Emergency Management

- **Mustering operations:** During emergency drills or real emergencies:
  - View muster station assignments
  - Track attendance at muster stations
  - Monitor who has reached their assigned muster point
  - See overall mustering progress
- **Acknowledge distress alarms:** Respond to emergency signals from personnel
- **Resolve distress situations:** Mark emergency situations as resolved once handled

### Filtering and Views

- **Toggle rescuers view:** Show/hide rescue personnel markers on the map
- **Show/hide empty nodes:** Control visibility of areas without personnel
- **Muster stations view:** Display designated muster stations on the map
- **Group vs Individual view:** Switch between viewing nodes as groups or individually

### Manual Personnel Management

- **Manual boarding actions:** Record embark/disembark actions for personnel when automatic detection isn't available
- **Update personnel status:** Manually adjust personnel locations if needed
