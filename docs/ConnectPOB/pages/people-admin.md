# People Admin Page

**Purpose:** Comprehensive personnel management system for registering, tracking, and managing all people aboard the vessel.

## What You Can Do:

### Personnel Registration

- **Add new personnel:** Register new crew members, passengers, or visitors
  - Enter personal details (name, role, company, shift)
  - Assign to groups/lists for better organization
  - Set cabin and bunk assignments
- **Import personnel data:** Bulk import from external systems

### Personnel Management

- **View all personnel:** Browse complete list of everyone on board
- **Search personnel:** Find specific people by name, role, wearable ID, cabin number, or company
- **Edit personnel details:** Update information for existing personnel
- **Delete personnel records:** Remove people who are no longer on board

### Wearable Device Management

- **Assign wearables:** Connect personnel to their tracking devices
- **Unassign wearables:** Remove wearable assignments when needed
- **Replace wearables:** Swap devices if they malfunction or get damaged
- **View wearable status:** See battery levels, signal strength, and connectivity

### Boarding Operations

- **Record embarkation:** Log when people board the vessel
- **Record disembarkation:** Log when people leave the vessel
- **Drop-off actions:** Handle temporary disembarkation (like shore excursions)
- **View boarding history:** See complete record of all boarding activities

### Organization and Filtering

- **Group management:** Create and manage personnel groups/lists
- **Filter by status:** View only embarked, disembarked, or dropped-off personnel
- **Filter by issues:** See personnel with unresolved problems or unassigned wearables
- **Sort personnel:** Order by name, role, last action, or other criteria

### Bulk Operations

- **Select multiple personnel:** Use checkboxes to select groups of people
- **Bulk boarding actions:** Process multiple people at once for embark/disembark
- **Bulk group assignments:** Assign multiple people to groups simultaneously
