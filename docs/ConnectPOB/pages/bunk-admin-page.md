# Bunk Admin Page

**Purpose:** Manage cabin and bunk assignments throughout the vessel, ensuring proper accommodation management and muster station assignments.

## What You Can Do:

### Bunk and Cabin Management

- **Add new bunks:** Create new bunk and cabin configurations
- **View all bunks:** See complete inventory of all bunks and cabins
- **Edit bunk details:** Modify bunk information, capacity, and settings
- **Delete bunks:** Remove bunks that are no longer available

### Accommodation Assignments

- **Assign personnel to bunks:** Connect crew members to their sleeping quarters
- **View occupancy:** See which bunks are occupied, available, or disabled
- **Multiple occupancy:** Handle bunks that can accommodate multiple people
- **Reassign accommodations:** Move personnel between bunks as needed

### Muster Station Configuration

- **Assign muster stations:** Connect each bunk to its designated emergency muster station
- **Update assignments:** Change muster station assignments when needed
- **View muster mapping:** See which bunks report to which muster stations

### Status Management

- **Enable/disable bunks:** Take bunks out of service for maintenance or other reasons
- **Track availability:** Monitor which bunks are available for new assignments
- **Occupancy monitoring:** See real-time occupancy status

### Search and Filtering

- **Search bunks:** Find specific bunks by cabin number, bunk ID, or occupant
- **Filter by status:** View only occupied, available, or disabled bunks
- **Filter by cabin type:** Show empty cabins, full cabins, or partially occupied cabins

### Bulk Operations

- **Select multiple bunks:** Use checkboxes to work with groups of bunks
- **Bulk status changes:** Enable/disable multiple bunks at once
- **Bulk muster assignments:** Assign multiple bunks to muster stations simultaneously

### Statistics and Overview

- **Occupancy statistics:** See counts of occupied, available, and disabled bunks
- **Visual indicators:** Color-coded display showing bunk status at a glance
- **Capacity planning:** Understand accommodation availability and usage
