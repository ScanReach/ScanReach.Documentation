# Information to the crew member

## Your Role in the ConnectPOB System

As a crew member, you play a crucial role in the effective operation of ConnectPOB:

### Daily Responsibilities

- Wear your assigned wearable device at all times when onboard
- Ensure your wearable is properly activated and functioning
- Follow proper procedures when embarking, disembarking, or temporarily leaving the vessel
- Report any issues with your wearable or the system to your supervisor

### Emergency Procedures

- Know how to activate the distress alarm on your wearable
- Understand what happens when a distress alarm is triggered
- Follow mustering procedures during drills and actual emergencies
- Assist others who may need help with their wearables during emergencies

### Maintenance & Care

- Protect your wearable from damage and extreme conditions
- Return your wearable to the designated location when leaving the vessel permanently

## Why ConnectPOB Matters to You

As a crew member, ConnectPOB directly impacts your safety and operational efficiency:

### Enhanced Safety

- In emergency situations, ConnectPOB provides an instant overview of all personnel onboard
- Your wearable allows you to trigger a distress alarm that immediately alerts the bridge and rescue teams
- The system helps rescue teams locate you quickly and accurately during emergencies
- During mustering, the system automatically accounts for personnel at designated muster stations

### Operational Efficiency

- Streamlined check-in/check-out procedures when embarking or disembarking
- Automated bunk management for living quarters
- Integration with existing crew management systems
- Reduced administrative workload for crew and officers

### Training & Preparedness

- Valuable tool during safety drills and training exercises
- Helps identify areas for improvement in emergency procedures
- Provides realistic practice scenarios for emergency response

## Your Safety is Our Priority

ConnectPOB is designed with one primary goal: to enhance the safety and security of everyone onboard your vessel. By understanding and properly using this system, you contribute to a safer working environment for yourself and your fellow crew members.

Thank you for taking the time to learn about ConnectPOB. Your commitment to safety and proper system usage is essential to making your vessel a safer place to work and live.
