# ConnectPOB Introduction

## Welcome to ConnectPOB

Welcome to the ConnectPOB system – your advanced onboard personnel management and safety solution designed specifically for maritime environments. As a crew member aboard your vessel, this system will serve as your essential companion for daily operations, emergency situations, and training exercises.

## What is ConnectPOB?

ConnectPOB is a state-of-the-art wireless system designed to manage people and their wearable devices onboard your vessel. It provides real-time information about personnel location, status, and safety, enabling quicker and better decision-making in critical situations. The system consists of:

- **Personal wearables** – Small devices carried by each crew member that transmit location and can trigger distress alarms
- **Vessel sensors** – Wireless mesh nodes placed throughout the vessel that detect and track wearables
- **Gateway system** – Central processing unit that collects and manages all data
- **Dashboard interface** – User-friendly displays accessible on the bridge and other key locations
- **Safety tablet** – Portable device for real-time monitoring and alarm management

## Getting Started

To begin using ConnectPOB:

1. **Receive your wearable** from the designated officer
2. **Complete the registration process** in the system
3. **Learn the basic functions** of your wearable device
4. **Understand the emergency procedures** specific to your vessel
5. **Ask questions** if anything is unclear – your safety depends on proper usage

## System Requirements

ConnectPOB operates as a "plug and play" solution on your vessel's existing Onboard Wireless Connectivity (OWC) system. The system is:

- **Wireless** – No cabling required between sensors
- **Battery-backed** – Continues to function during power blackouts
- **User-friendly** – Designed for easy operation by all crew members
- **Secure** – All data is protected and only accessible to authorized users

## General Navigation Tips

### Common Interface Elements

- **Search bars:** Use to quickly find specific items across all pages
- **Filter toggles:** Use checkboxes to show/hide different types of content
- **Bulk selection:** Use checkboxes to select multiple items for group operations
- **Floating action buttons:** Quick access to common actions like "Add" or "Handle"
- **Two-column layout:** Most pages have a list view and detail panel
- **Top bar:** Contains search, filters, and page-specific controls

### Mobile/Tablet Adaptations

- Many pages automatically adapt for mobile and tablet use
- Mobile interfaces may show fewer columns or simplified views
- Touch-friendly buttons and controls
- Swipe gestures where appropriate

### Emergency Situations

- During mustering events, some features may be disabled or modified
- Safety page becomes the primary interface during emergencies
- Critical safety functions remain accessible at all times
- System prioritizes safety operations during emergency scenarios
